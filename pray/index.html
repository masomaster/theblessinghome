Pray for the Blessing Home

“Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in all circumstances; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.” (1 Thessalonians 5:16–18, NRSV)

Please join us in praying for the following prayer requests:

1. A Permanent Home

For 23 years the children have lived in rented houses, moving whenever owners needed the property. Praise God that Blessing Home has purchased a piece of land and begun foundation work. The estimated cost to build the home is ₹1.5 crore. Pray for the funds to complete a home where 22 orphaned and abandoned children can live without the fear of being uprooted.

2. School Fees

Paying school fees at the start of each academic year is a great challenge. Pray that God would provide so every child can continue in English-medium school and become self-sufficient.

3. Family Support

Pray for the families Blessing Home helps in Bangalore and KGF with monthly provisions and school fees, including families facing illness, blindness, and loss of work.

4. Graduates

Praise God for students who have finished their studies, found work, and are living independently — including those now teaching, working, and studying abroad. Pray for their continued guidance, protection, and godly marriages.

5. Passing on the Blessing

Pray for our graduates, and for all of the children, to serve the Lord in all that they do regardless of their occupations, and be a blessing to those around them in every circumstance.

6. Spiritual Walk

Please pray that the children continue to grow in their spiritual walk with God and that they are fulfilled in Christ.

7. Volunteers and Donors

Please pray for many more volunteers who can speak life into our children, and for the donors whose generosity makes this work possible.

(Updated March 2025)
